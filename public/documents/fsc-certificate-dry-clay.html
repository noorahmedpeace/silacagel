OFFICIAL DECLARATION: DryGelWorld (Kamran Enterprises) hereby certifies that the breathable Kraft Paper packaging sachets, pouches, and corrugated master shipping cartons used for its Industrial Dry Clay Desiccant (Activated Bentonite / Montmorillonite) are manufactured exclusively from FSC-certified raw wood-pulp substrates originating from responsibly managed, sustainable forests in full compliance with FSC-STD-40-004 V3-1 (Chain of Custody). The production, conversion, and dispatch workflows operate under DryGelWorld's certified ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System (Cert #9101225) with complete batch lot traceability.
