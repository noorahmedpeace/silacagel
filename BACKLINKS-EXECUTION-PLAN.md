# DryGelWorld — Backlinks & Authority Execution Plan

**Why this is the #1 priority:** the site's on-page SEO is already strong (topical clusters, comparison content, clean metadata, structured data). GSC shows content ranking at positions 5–16 with impressions but few clicks. That profile = a **new, low-authority domain**. The lever that moves it is **backlinks + citations** (Google trust), not more on-page tweaks. Do this consistently for 8–12 weeks and rankings climb.

**Rules:** use the SAME business name, address, phone (NAP) everywhere. Always link to `https://www.drygelworld.com`. Quality > quantity — a few relevant links beat dozens of spammy ones.

---

## ⚠️ 2026-09-02 UPDATE — what actually moves Domain Rating

Ahrefs snapshot (2026-09-02): **DR 19 · 721 backlinks · 386 linking websites · only 7% of linking domains dofollow (~27 domains).**

Ahrefs DR is computed **only from dofollow referring domains**, weighted by each linker's own DR. The citation work in Weeks 1–3 below is done and was worth doing (NAP trust, leads, AI-citation surfaces) — but directories, marketplaces, social profiles, Quora/Reddit and forum links are **nofollow** and contribute **zero** to DR. Adding more of them pads the 721 number and leaves DR at 19.

**From here on, the only work that counts toward DR is an editorial dofollow link from a site with DR 30+.** The weekly cadence below has been rewritten around that rule. Directory/citation work is now maintenance only (fix a broken listing, add a new marketplace if it brings leads).

**Definition of a "counting" link (all four must be true):**
1. `rel` attribute has no `nofollow`, `ugc` or `sponsored` (check with the Ahrefs SEO Toolbar or right-click → Inspect the anchor).
2. Linking domain DR ≥ 30 (Ahrefs free authority checker: ahrefs.com/website-authority-checker).
3. Placed in body content or a curated resources list, not a footer, sidebar, profile or comment.
4. Not paid, not reciprocal-by-arrangement, not on a site that sells links.

**Target:** +8–12 counting links per month. Roughly 40–60 counting links at DR 30+ moves DR 19 → ~30 (4–6 months at that pace). DR is logarithmic; 30 → 40 takes considerably longer.

**Target lists:** `OUTREACH-HITLIST-2026-07.md` (30 editorial targets, verified) and `COMPETITOR-LINK-INTERSECT-2026-09-02.md` (pages already linking to competitors — highest conversion, pitch these first).

## ONGOING (every week) — dofollow-only tactics, ranked by DR payoff

**Weekly quota: 10 personalised pitches, 3 journalist replies, 1 published piece per month. That is the whole job. Nothing else on this list counts.**

### 1. Link-intersect outreach (highest conversion) — 5 pitches/week
Pages that already link to Absortech, Clariant, Desiccare, Multisorb, WiseSorbent etc. have proven they link out to desiccant suppliers. Work `COMPETITOR-LINK-INTERSECT-2026-09-02.md` top to bottom. Angle: "you link to X's calculator/guide; here is a free, ungated alternative with [specific extra: DIN 55474 method / metric + imperial / FOB Karachi cost line]". One follow-up at day 7, then stop.

### 2. Resource-page pitches for the two calculators — 3 pitches/week
The Container Desiccant Calculator and Moisture Load Calculator are the only true link magnets on the site. Search operators to find fresh targets (log each one in the SENT LOG so it is never re-pitched):
```
"desiccant" intitle:resources
"container rain" intitle:links OR intitle:resources
"cargo moisture" "useful links"
"packaging calculators" OR "logistics calculators" -site:drygelworld.com
"moisture-sensitive" "useful tools" OR "resources"
site:.edu "silica gel" "suppliers"
site:.org "desiccant" "resources"
```
Resource-page links are almost always dofollow and rarely removed.

### 3. Journalist-request platforms — 3 replies/week (Qwoted, Featured, SourceBottle, MentionMatch)
Signups and pre-cleared quotes are in `OUTREACH-HITLIST-2026-07.md` §4 and `OUTREACH-KIT.md` §2. Filter for packaging, shipping, supply chain, moisture, humidity, manufacturing, export, Pakistan. Answer within 2 hours of the query (first replies win), 80–150 words, one concrete number, no product pitch. These land DR 50–90 editorial links and are the most efficient DR tactic available to a small team.

### 4. One original-data asset per quarter (the compounding play)
Journalists and bloggers link to data, not product pages. One good study can earn 10–30 dofollow domains on its own and feeds tactic 3 for months. Candidates, in order:
- **"Container humidity by trade lane"** — log temperature/RH inside real shipments Karachi → Jebel Ali / Hamburg / Rotterdam / Shanghai with a $30 USB data logger, publish charts + downloadable CSV + methodology. Nobody has published this for South-Asia-origin lanes.
- **"Desiccant unit calculator benchmark"** — run the same 20 shipments through Clariant DESI PAK, Desiccare WVTR, Sorbent Systems and the DGW calculator; publish the spread and explain why the methods differ.
- **"Cobalt chloride in indicating silica gel: a 2026 market survey"** — buy 15 "orange/blue" gels on Alibaba/Amazon, test for cobalt, publish results (REACH SVHC angle; EU trade press will cover it).
Promote each one with tactic 1 and 2 pitches and a Qwoted/Featured "we have new data" note.

### 5. Guest posts on DR 40+ trade publications — 1 per month
Full pitches for Packaging Digest, Inbound Logistics, Pharmaceutical Online are in `OUTREACH-KIT.md` §3; outlines in §4; further "write for us" targets in `OUTREACH-HITLIST-2026-07.md` §2–3. Byline or in-body link only. Confirm the publication gives dofollow author links **before** writing (check any recent guest post on the site with the Ahrefs toolbar).

### 6. Partner and customer links — batch once a month
Every distributor, freight forwarder, packaging reseller, testing lab and trade body DGW works with has a "partners", "suppliers" or "members" page. Ask specifically: *"Could you add DryGelWorld to your suppliers page with a link to https://www.drygelworld.com?"* Offer a reciprocal mention on the DGW "Partners" page only if they ask (do not make it a condition). Easiest editorial links available.

### 7. Unlinked mentions and broken-link reclaim — 30 min a month
- Google: `"drygelworld" -site:drygelworld.com`, `"dry gel world"`, `"Kamran Enterprises" silica gel` → email every page that mentions without linking.
- Ahrefs (free): Site Explorer → Backlinks → filter "nofollow" and DR ≥ 40 → email the 5 best asking for the link to be made followed (works surprisingly often on blogs and trade bodies).
- Ahrefs Broken Backlinks on competitors (absortech.com, desiccare.com, multisorb.com): any dead competitor page that has links → pitch the equivalent live DGW page to each linker.

### Do NOT spend time on (nofollow, zero DR effect)
Directories, marketplaces, business-profile sites, Quora, Reddit, forum signatures, blog comments, Medium/LinkedIn articles, press-release distribution (PRWeb etc. are nofollow), web 2.0 sites, "guest post packages" or any paid link. Buying links risks a Google penalty and Ahrefs discounts them anyway.
